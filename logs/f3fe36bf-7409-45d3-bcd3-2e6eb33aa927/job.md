Create an automated daily financial report that includes:

1. **Market Overview**
   - Major index performance (S&P 500, Dow Jones, NASDAQ, international markets)
   - Daily changes and percentage moves
   - Market sentiment indicators

2. **Economic Data**
   - Key economic releases for the day
   - Upcoming economic calendar events
   - Currency movements (USD, EUR, JPY, GBP)

3. **Sector Analysis**
   - Top performing sectors
   - Worst performing sectors
   - Notable sector news and trends

4. **Notable Movers**
   - Top gainers and losers
   - High volume stocks
   - Significant corporate news affecting stock prices

5. **Market News Summary**
   - Key financial news headlines
   - Central bank announcements
   - Geopolitical events affecting markets

6. **Technical Indicators**
   - VIX (volatility index)
   - Bond yields (10-year Treasury)
   - Commodity prices (oil, gold)

The report should be formatted as a professional daily briefing document and saved to the logs directory with today's date. Use reliable financial data sources and present the information in a clear, concise manner suitable for investment professionals.
